## Context

REQ-005 offered REQ-003's readable durations to upstream as
[PR #4785](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/pull/4785). The maintainer answered,
and closed it:

> hi, thanks for the PR. While I like the feature I think its a lot more complex than it
> need to be. I do not think we need the user preference. Just show the times in a human
> readable format.
>
> Alternatively one could change the field to a proper duration field, that would likely
> make a lot more sense but require a migration of existing data.
>
> Closing this for now, feel free to implement in the recommended way or I will look into
> this at some point.

This is the follow-on REQ that REQ-005's Notes named ("Responding to review feedback … is a
named follow-on REQ, to be intaken if and when feedback arrives"). REQ-005 itself stays
`done`: it completed at "PR open and CI green" by Decision 11, deliberately so that no REQ
of this fork could be parked on someone else's review queue.

**The feature was accepted; the mechanism was not.** He is not asking for a different
formatter, a different format rule, or different call sites — all of that survives
untouched. He is asking for one thing to be deleted: `UserPreference.use_readable_time`.
Deleting it is not a trim, it removes the PR's entire backend half:

| On `readable-durations` today | Under the recommended shape |
|---|---|
| `cookbook/migrations/0243_userpreference_use_readable_time.py` | gone — the branch touches **no** `cookbook/` path at all |
| `cookbook/models.py`, `cookbook/serializer.py` | gone |
| `cookbook/tests/api/test_api_userpreference.py` (upstream's file, appended to) | gone |
| `vue3/src/openapi/models/{UserPreference,PatchedUserPreference}.ts` | gone — no regeneration, no stale-client risk |
| `vue3/src/components/settings/CosmeticSettings.vue` | gone |
| `vue3/src/locales/en.json` — `h`, `Use_Readable_Time`, `Use_Readable_Time_Help` | one key, `h` |
| `vue3/src/utils/duration_utils.ts` | stays, minus the `useReadableTime` parameter |
| `vue3/src/composables/useDurationDisplay.ts` | stays, binding i18n labels only |
| the three display components | stay |
| the vitest commit | stays |

Eleven files become five. Three of REQ-005's own risk areas disappear with them: the
migration-number collision it had to re-check when cutting the branch, the OpenAPI
regeneration trap that CLAUDE.md warns about, and the default-value question that
Decisions 2 and 3 spent most of their length on — he is explicitly asking for the display
change to be unconditional, which is the very thing a default-OFF preference existed to
avoid.

**The fork simplifies with it.** REQ-003 Decision 2 chose the preference because
`use_fractions` was a worn seam; Decision 3 defaulted it ON because a display improvement
behind an unknown setting is not delivered. Both were right for a fork acting alone, and
both are now liabilities: a fork that keeps a preference the upstream version does not have
will conflict with its own contribution the moment it lands, for a toggle this household has
never turned off. Removing it locally shrinks this REQ's divergence from upstream to a
handful of frontend lines (REQ-001 Decision 5) and makes the PR branch a replay of code
that has already run in the household's own kitchen rather than a construction that exists
only to be reviewed.

**The two trees are not identical, and that matters when cutting the branch.** REQ-008
rewrote `StepView.vue` to show per-step working and waiting time, so the fork now has six
duration display sites where upstream's tree has four. The PR branch is therefore *not* a
cherry-pick of the fork's `StepView.vue`; it carries the pre-REQ-008 shape of that file with
the preference argument removed. Everything else — the formatter, the composable,
`RecipeView.vue`, `RecipeCard.vue`, `en.json`, the spec — replays cleanly.

**What dies with the preference here.** Three of REQ-003's named acceptance tests grade a
field that will no longer exist: `cookbook/tests/api/test_api_user_preference.py` (AC2, the
round-trip), `cookbook/tests/other/test_openapi_client_fresh.py` (AC3, the generated client
exposes `useReadableTime`), and `cookbook/tests/other/test_readable_time_signoff.py` (AC4's
grader, half of which transcribes what the surfaces read *with the preference off*). They
are deleted, not adapted — there is nothing left for them to assert.

## Requirement

Readable durations are unconditional — in this fork and in a new pull request offered
upstream in the shape the maintainer asked for.

### The fork

`UserPreference.use_readable_time` no longer exists: the model field, its serializer entry,
the `Use_Readable_Time` and `Use_Readable_Time_Help` keys in `en.json`, the Cosmetic
settings checkbox, and the `useReadableTime` property in the generated `UserPreference` and
`PatchedUserPreference` client models are all gone, with a migration dropping the column
that orders after REQ-008's `0244`. `formatDuration` loses its `useReadableTime` parameter
and `useDurationDisplay` stops reading the user-preference store; both keep their shape
otherwise, and all six display sites — both `RecipeView` layouts, the `RecipeCard` chip and
REQ-008's three `StepView` readings — render exactly as they do today with the preference
on. `cookbook/tests/api/test_api_user_preference.py`,
`cookbook/tests/other/test_openapi_client_fresh.py` and
`cookbook/tests/other/test_readable_time_signoff.py` are deleted.

### The pull request branch

`readable-durations-v2`, cut from a freshly fetched `upstream/develop`, carrying the
unconditional formatter and nothing else, as two commits:

1. `vue3/src/utils/duration_utils.ts`, `vue3/src/composables/useDurationDisplay.ts`, the
   `h` key in `vue3/src/locales/en.json`, and the four upstream display sites
   (`RecipeView.vue` ×2 layouts, `RecipeCard.vue`, `StepView.vue` in its **pre-REQ-008**
   shape);
2. the vitest introduction — `vue3/package.json`, `vue3/vitest.config.ts`,
   `vue3/yarn.lock`, `vue3/src/utils/duration_utils.spec.ts` — as the branch tip.

The branch's diff against `upstream/develop` touches **no** path under `cookbook/`,
`vue3/src/openapi/`, `requirements/`, `.devsteward/`, `.claude/`, `.github/` or `deploy/`,
no `STEWARD.md` and no `CLAUDE.md`, adds no migration, and touches no locale catalog but
`en.json`. The word `use_readable_time` appears nowhere in it.

### The proof before publishing

A develop-session obligation, not a criterion routed to validation: on the fork, the full
Python suite and `yarn --cwd vue3 test` pass and `makemigrations --check` is clean; the
change is deployed to the household instance with `deploy/deploy.sh` and iterated there
until AC5's surfaces read correctly. On the branch, the vitest suite, `yarn build` and the
flake8 / isort / yapf / prettier gate `guidelines.md` requires pass on every changed file,
and pushing to `origin` runs this fork's own workflows before the PR is opened.

## Notes

**Required environment.** AC2 needs the vue3 toolchain — Node ≥ 20.19, yarn, and
`yarn install` having been run in `vue3/` — established by REQ-003 and present on this
workstation; it hard-fails rather than skips when absent. AC3 and AC4 read git state a clean
checkout does not have: the `upstream` remote fetched, and both branches present locally.
That is environment-bound in the sense REQ-064 describes and is recorded here deliberately
rather than left to surface as a silent skip. The oracle is coupled — this repository's own
git history — so they stay `regression`, exactly as REQ-005's equivalents did; the develop
session's first act is `git fetch upstream`. Publication additionally needs `gh`
authenticated as `dr-froehlich`.

**Why the live proofs sit in develop, not in validation.** Both the deploy and the push
only produce something observable *after* an outward action, and both need a repair loop —
a red deploy or a red workflow has to be fixed by the session that made it. The System
Tester runs in a fresh session that never sees the diff and cannot fix anything, so routing
"it deployed and it runs" or "CI is green" there would turn every red into a lost session.
AC5 and AC6 keep only what genuinely needs a human oracle and no fixing loop: reading a
page against a checklist.

**Superseded, and where the record lives.** REQ-003 Decision 2 (a per-user preference
mirroring `use_fractions`) and Decision 3 (defaulting ON) are superseded by Decision 3 here.
REQ-003's AC2 and AC3, and the preference-off half of its AC4, become unrunnable when their
tests are deleted — `steward revalidate REQ-003` will not work afterwards, which is the
honest consequence of the feature they graded no longer existing. REQ-003 is not edited
(Decision 7); this paragraph is the record. REQ-005 is unaffected: its branch, its ACs and
its evidence all describe a pull request that really was opened and really was reviewed.

**Accepted data loss.** The removal migration drops a boolean column. Nothing records what
any user had set, and the reverse migration cannot restore it — it recreates the column at
its `True` default. On this instance every account has it at the default, so the loss is
nominal; it is stated because it is real.

**Known gap, homed not deferred — the `h` key.** Unchanged from REQ-005. The hour unit is
still English-only in 35 of 36 catalogs and falls back to English under
`fallbackLocale: 'en'`. Upstream's `translations.md` asks that translations come through
Weblate, so this REQ still ships the English key alone; the home is Weblate's normal cadence
once the key is merged. Nothing is required in this fork — the fallback `h` is correct
German. Removing `Use_Readable_Time` and `Use_Readable_Time_Help` from `en.json` shrinks the
gap by two keys, since those two are the ones Weblate would otherwise have had to carry for
a setting that no longer exists.

**Out of scope.** Converting `working_time`/`waiting_time` to a duration field (Decision 2)
— if the maintainer builds it, adapting this fork to it is a REQ to intake then. Duration
*entry* stays in minutes across all editors. Merge timing and any further review feedback
are out of scope by REQ-001 Decision 3, exactly as they were for REQ-005; another round of
feedback is another follow-on REQ. REQ-004's bake schedule is still not offered upstream.
