## Context

REQ-003 made durations readable: a 72-hour proof now says `72 h` instead of `4320 min`.
That answered *how long*, and immediately exposed the question it could not answer — **when**.
For a bread that spans three days, "72 h" is still a number the baker has to do arithmetic
on, backwards, in their head, against a wall clock, for every step in the recipe. The
question a baker actually asks is never "how long is the bulk ferment", it is "if I want
bread on Sunday morning, when do I mix the levain".

Today nothing in Tandoor answers that. `Step.time` (`models.py:966`) is a bare integer of
minutes with no anchor to a clock. The only absolute time anywhere in the recipe display is
`Timer.vue`'s "Finished at" line, which requires the cook to have *started* a countdown —
useless for planning, since planning happens the day before. And the meal plan already
holds the one datetime that matters: `MealPlan.from_date` (`models.py:1277`) is a real
`DateTimeField` carrying a time of day, seeded from `MealType.time` or defaulting to 12:00
(`MealPlanEditor.vue:224`). That datetime is *already* semantically a finishing time — it
is when the meal is eaten — and it is currently thrown away the moment you open the recipe.

Two clocks exist on a recipe and they do not agree. `Recipe.working_time` and
`Recipe.waiting_time` are author-curated fields, independent of the steps and never derived
from them; the sum of `Step.time` across the step list is a separate number. The deployed
Pizza recipe demonstrates the gap — waiting time 2400 min against a longest single step of
1440 min. Only one of the two can drive a schedule, and only the step chain has the
per-step resolution the feature needs.

Three things make this cheap. The recipe detail page already has a precedent for a
value-editing control hanging off the times row: `recipe-scaling-dialog` sits inside the
servings column in *both* the mobile and desktop layouts of `RecipeView.vue`. The two
planner-to-recipe entry points already carry state through the URL — `servings` travels as
a query param from `HorizontalMealPlanWindow.clickMealPlan()` (`:152`) and from
`RecipeCard.openRecipe()` (`:126`), the latter being what the meal plan entry dialog uses
(`MealPlanEditor.vue:32`). And REQ-003 installed vitest, so the scheduling arithmetic —
another pure function — is testable the day it is written.

One thing is genuinely latent rather than solved: **sub-recipes**. A step may point at
another whole recipe via `Step.step_recipe`, which `StepView.vue:44` renders as nested
steps. The parent step keeps its own independent `time`. A back-chain that does not recurse
counts only that parent `time`, so a levain sub-recipe taking 12 h under a parent step left
at 0 makes every earlier start time silently *late* — wrong, not merely absent. This
household has never used sub-recipes, so the defect is latent today; Decision 7 refuses to
let it stay silent anyway.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|----------|-----------|
| 1 | The schedule is a **backward chain over `Step.time` only**. The last step *ends* at the finish time; each step's start is the following step's start minus that step's own time. `working_time`/`waiting_time` play no part | The two clocks disagree in the normal case and nothing reconciles them. Only the step chain has per-step resolution, which is the entire point. Deriving from the recipe total would produce a schedule that contradicts the step times printed beside it |
| 2 | A step with `time` 0 shows the same start as the step that follows it | Zero-duration steps are prep notes and headers; giving them their own row of clock time would be noise, and giving them nothing would leave gaps in the column |
| 3 | The finish time lives **only in the URL** as a query param; there is no persistence, no model, no migration, no API change | `servings` already proves the seam. Clearing the param *is* requirement 3 ("remove it again"). Server-side storage would cost a migration, serializer work and an OpenAPI client regeneration — which in this checkout is the ~40-plugin-file trap CLAUDE.md documents — for a value whose natural lifetime is one bake |
| 4 | The manual control is a **weekday + time** picker resolving to the next upcoming occurrence of that weekday; there is no calendar widget | Bakers plan against "Sunday breakfast", not against 2026-08-16. A weekday picker is two taps where a date picker is four, and it cannot express a wrong year |
| 5 | A finish time arriving from the planner is honoured **exactly**, at any horizon, overriding the picker's 7-day reach. Touching the picker re-snaps to the next occurrence and overrides the planner value | The planner is authoritative about a planned meal — a bake planned three weeks out must not be silently clamped. The picker is a manual-entry affordance, not a constraint on the value |
| 6 | Start times render as **weekday + time**, with `Today`/`Tomorrow` for the near cases and an explicit day offset (`Sat 18:30 (−9 d)`) once the span exceeds a week | A bare weekday is ambiguous beyond seven days, which sourdough starter builds reach. The offset keeps the weekday shape while making a misread impossible |
| 7 | Sub-recipe steps are **not** recursed into, but the case is **detected and flagged**: a step carrying a `step_recipe` whose own `time` is 0 puts a visible "schedule incomplete" notice on the page | Refusing to recurse keeps the diff small and avoids designing for a feature this household has never used. Flagging means the schedule is never *silently* wrong — the fix is one integer in the step editor, since a parent step can simply carry the sub-recipe's total. Full recursion is homed in a named follow-on REQ (see Notes) |
| 8 | The control is a **fourth column in the times row**, mirroring `recipe-scaling-dialog`'s placement in the servings column | The precedent lands the control in both `RecipeView` layouts for free, keeps the mobile layout intact, and puts the finish time beside the durations it schedules |
| 9 | Weekday and time names come from **luxon's locale formatting**, not from new translation keys; only the handful of literal labels get keys, English only | Weekday names are already localized by the runtime — inventing seven translation keys would be worse in every language. CLAUDE.md forbids hand-editing non-English catalogs; Weblate fills the rest |
| 10 | No change to any editor, serializer, or the API | Scheduling is a display concern derived from data that already exists. Keeping the backend untouched keeps this rebase-clean per REQ-001 Decision 2 |

## Requirement

A baker can state when a recipe must be finished and read, per step, when that step has to
start.

### The scheduler

A shared pure function takes a finish datetime and an ordered list of step durations in
minutes, and returns a start datetime per step:

- The **last** step ends at the finish time; its start is `finish − its own time`.
- Each earlier step's start is the **following step's start** minus its own time.
- A step with time 0, null or undefined starts when the following step starts.
- The **overall start** is the first step's start.
- An empty step list, or a recipe whose every step has time 0, yields an overall start
  equal to the finish time and no per-step offsets.

### The finish control

A fourth column in the recipe detail times row, present in both the mobile and desktop
layouts of `RecipeView.vue`, alongside working time, waiting time and servings. Unset, it
offers to set a finish time. Set, it shows the computed **overall start** and offers to
change or clear it. The dialog behind it picks a **weekday and a time**, resolving to the
next upcoming occurrence of that weekday; it carries an explicit clear action.

Setting a finish time writes it to the URL as a query param; clearing it removes the param
and, with it, every absolute time on the page — the display returns exactly to what
REQ-003 left. The param survives a reload and makes the scheduled recipe a shareable link.

### The step display

With a finish time set, every step shows its start as **weekday + time**: `Today 18:30`,
`Tomorrow 06:00`, `Sat 18:30`, and `Sat 18:30 (−9 d)` once the step starts more than six
days before the finish. With no finish time set, no step shows any absolute time.

Start times appear in print mode, so a bake schedule can be printed and taped to the wall.

### The planner hand-off

Opening a recipe from the planner carries the planned meal's `from_date` in as the finish
time, at both entry points that exist: the start page meal-plan strip
(`HorizontalMealPlanWindow.clickMealPlan`) and the meal plan entry dialog's embedded recipe
card (`MealPlanEditor.vue` → `RecipeCard.openRecipe`). The planner value is used verbatim
whatever its horizon. Once on the page it behaves like any other finish time — changeable
and clearable.

### The sub-recipe guard

When any step carries a `step_recipe` and its own `time` is 0, the page states that the
schedule is incomplete and names the step, so no start time on the page is read as
authoritative when it cannot be.

## Notes

**Required environment (AC1–AC4).** Same as REQ-003 AC1: Node ≥ 20.19 and yarn, with
`yarn install` having run in `vue3/`. REQ-003's develop session installed that toolchain,
so it is present; the ACs hard-fail rather than skip if it regresses. AC5 is graded from
evidence captured against the deployment.

**Why AC5 is `manual` and not deferred iteration.** The arithmetic is exhaustively covered
by AC1–AC4 against synthetic tables — there is no production corpus that can surprise a
pure function of a datetime and a list of integers. AC5 checks *wiring*: that the control
reaches both `RecipeView` layouts in a built bundle, that clearing really clears, and that
the planner hand-off survives the round trip through a query param. That needs a human
oracle but no fixing loop, so the System-Test phase is the right home. The operator creates
the meal plan entry as part of setting up the observable state; the System Tester never
sees the diff and must not be left inventing fixtures.

**Known defect, homed not deferred (Decision 7).** Sub-recipe steps produce an incomplete
schedule. This REQ makes that visible rather than silent. Full recursion into
`step_recipe` step lists — including deciding whether a parent step's own `time` adds to or
overrides the nested total — is a follow-on REQ, to be intaken the first time this
household actually builds a recipe out of sub-recipes. Until then the workaround is exact
and one field wide: set the parent step's `time` to the sub-recipe's total.

**Scope boundaries.** No model, migration, serializer or API change, and therefore no
OpenAPI client regeneration. Nothing is written back to the meal plan — the hand-off is
one-way, and adjusting a bake's schedule never edits the planned meal. The meal plan
calendar item and dialog do **not** show a required start time; that would need step data
the meal plan store does not fetch, and is out of scope. Share-link recipe views are out of
scope. `Timer.vue`'s countdown and its "+Xd" finish line are already correct and untouched.
Duration *entry* stays in minutes everywhere, as REQ-003 left it.

**Upstream.** Not part of this REQ (REQ-001 Decision 3). The strategy chosen at intake is
to offer REQ-003 to issue #382 on its own first — it is small, and a maintainer already
agreed to it in 2021 — and to offer this separately later, after it has run in the
household's own kitchen. Decisions 3 and 10 keep the diff frontend-only and rebase-clean so
that stays possible.
